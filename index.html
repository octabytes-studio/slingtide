The game

A tiny beach. A very big problem.

Tully has one job: keep the nest safe. The beach critters have other plans. Every shot is a small decision — aim, power, angle, and which critter to pop first.

One thumb, any angle

Touch anywhere and drag. A full draw hits harder and punches through; walls turn a clever angle into a bank shot.

Fifty levels, five shores

Travel from Sunny Cove to Shipwreck Shore. Every shore ends in a boss, and every level has three stars to chase.

Five bosses, five phases

Queen Moon Snail, Box Crab, Grand Octopus, Mirage Cuttlefish and the Coconut Crab King each change the fight as their health falls.

Thirteen upgrades

Spend shells in the Workshop across the launcher, base, shots and helpers. Unlock five projectiles, from Sea Glass to Beam Glass.

Every critter shows its HP

Hermit Crabs shrug off light hits. Fiddler Crabs block from the front. Starfish heal, Big Conchs split, Seagulls hover. The pips tell you what a shot will do.

Made for one hand

Portrait, short sessions, fully offline. Reduce Motion, bigger HP pips, a left-handed HUD and colour assist are all in Settings.

How to play

Aim. Release. Hold the fence.

No tutorials to read, no timers to beat. Just a slingshot, a beach full of critters, and Tully behind you.

Aim anywhere

Touch anywhere on the beach and drag. The slingshot follows your thumb, so you never cover the target.

Release to fire

A full draw hits harder and pierces. On later shores, walls turn a clever angle into a bank shot.

Hold the line

Pop every critter before it reaches the fence. Clear the level, earn shells, then spend them in the Workshop.

Questions

The short answers.

If yours is not here, email us at octabytes.studio@gmail.com.

When can I play Slingtide?

Slingtide is coming to the App Store and Google Play. This page will link to both listings as soon as they are live. There is no download link yet, and we will not send you to another game's store page.

Is it free?

The price will be shown on the store listing when the game launches. The current version does not sell in-app purchases, so there is no shop, no subscription and no paywall inside the game.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Gameplay, progress, settings and achievements all work without a connection. Ads need a network; if you are offline, the game carries on without them.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, no cloud save and no social profile. Your progress is stored on your device and in your own device backup.

Are there ads?

Yes — Google AdMob. Banners appear on menu screens, one interstitial can appear between a run and its results, and two optional rewarded ads can offer a continue or double currency. Ads never cover a run. Players under 13 (and unknown ages) get non-personalized banners only; 13–17s get non-personalized ads; adults may get personalized ads only with consent. Full details.

Can I remove ads?

The current version does not sell an ad-removal purchase. If we add one, it will use the App Store or Google Play billing and these terms will be updated before it ships.

What devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 15 or later, and Android phones and tablets on Android 7.0 or later. The game is portrait-only and designed for one hand. Large-tablet layout is still being polished; check the store listing for the final device list.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account to delete. Open Settings → Reset progress and type ERASE to clear levels, stars, shells, upgrades, achievements, stats and the age band. To remove everything, including settings and the random player ID, uninstall the app and remove it from your device backup. Step-by-step instructions.

How do I report a bug or ask a privacy question?

Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with your device, OS version and what happened. For game bugs, Settings → Copy diagnostics puts the build details on your clipboard so you can paste them into the email. Contact page.
