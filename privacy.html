The short version

  • Slingtide has no accounts. We do not receive your game progress, player ID or age band.
  • Your save stays on your device and may be included in your own device backup.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and ad data as described below. We do not sell personal information.
  • You can delete local data with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the game. We hold no server copy to delete.
  • The game has no analytics, crash-reporting or purchase SDK. This website has no cookies or analytics.
  • Players under 13, and players of unknown age, get non-personalized banners only.

1. Who we are

Slingtide is developed and published by OctaBytes (“OctaBytes”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle information when you play the Slingtide game for iOS or Android, or visit this website.

Privacy contact: octabytes.studio@gmail.com. Use the subject line “Privacy request” so it reaches the right place. We do not have a separate Data Protection Officer, and we have not appointed an EU or UK representative. If that changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

2. Scope and dates

This policy covers the Slingtide game on iOS and Android and the Slingtide website at octabytes-studio.github.io/slingtide. It does not cover the App Store, Google Play, your device's backup service, or any other app or website. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Effective date: November 7, 2026. Last updated: November 7, 2026. Version: 1.0.

5. Game data on your device

Slingtide keeps one save document in the app's private support folder. It writes a temporary file, keeps the previous good copy as save.bak, and replaces the live file atomically. The save contains your progress, wallet, upgrades, loadout, achievements, lifetime stats, settings, onboarding state, the random player ID and the derived age band.

We do not operate a server that receives this save. We cannot read it, restore it or move it between devices. If your device is lost, the save is lost with it unless your own device backup has a copy.

Device backup. Android Auto Backup and iCloud backup are operated by Google and Apple under your settings. If you enable them, a copy of the save may be included. You can turn backup off or delete the backup in your device settings; we cannot do that for you.

Reset progress. Settings → Reset progress → type ERASE clears levels, stars, shells, upgrades, achievements, stats, onboarding, recent-run history and the age band. It keeps your settings, the random player ID and the purchases block (empty in this version). To remove those too, uninstall the app.

Diagnostics. Settings → Copy diagnostics puts a build footer and the app's recent log lines on your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it into an email or message yourself. The footer includes the player ID and app version; remove anything you do not want to share before sending.

Share card and review prompt. The results share button composes an image and opens the system share sheet. We receive nothing back. The rate prompt uses the platform's in-app review API; we do not receive your review, name or account.

6. Ads, consent and tracking

Slingtide shows ads from Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process the data listed in the table above. The game itself does not send your save, player ID, age band as a profile, or gameplay records to us or to Google.

Formats. An anchored banner can appear on menu screens. One interstitial can appear behind the curtain between the end of a run and the results screen. Two optional rewarded ads can offer a continue after a fail or double the run's currency. Ads never appear over the launcher or during a run, and a rewarded ad only grants its reward if you finish it.

Personalization. Only adults (18 or over) may receive personalized ads, and only with consent where the law requires it. Teens (13–17) receive non-personalized ads. Children under 13, and players of unknown age, receive non-personalized banners only — no interstitial, no rewarded ad, no consent form and no tracking prompt.

Consent. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and regulated US states, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent before personalized ads. Where the law requires it, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. The entry only appears when Google says a privacy-options form is required.

iOS tracking. On iOS, adults may be asked for permission to track across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency) after consent. If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used for personalized ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change the permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Minors are never shown the ATT prompt.

Declining. You can decline personalized ads and still play. Ads may be less relevant, and some ad formats may not be available. We do not block the game based on your ad choice.

Google's own privacy information: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

No sale. We do not sell personal information for money. Our ad partner may share data with its advertising partners as described in Google's privacy policy. See the California notice for how to opt out of personalized advertising.

7. Children and families

Slingtide is a general-audience game that can be played by families. It has no accounts, no chat, no public profiles and no user-generated content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 on our systems.

When ads are enabled, the game asks for a birth month and year once. It uses the answer only to derive an age band, then discards the date. Only the band is stored on the device. Under-13 and unknown ages are treated as children: non-personalized banners only, no interstitial, no rewarded ad, no UMP form and no ATT prompt. We configure the ad SDK for child-directed treatment for those players. Google may still process limited ad data to serve and measure those non-personalized banners, as described in section 6.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact octabytes.studio@gmail.com and we will delete that message. We cannot delete data from a child's device remotely; use Reset progress or uninstall the app, and manage the device backup in the OS settings.

We do not claim to be “COPPA compliant” or “kidSAFE certified”. We describe what the game actually does so you and a reviewer can check it.

8. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message, including attachments and any player ID or diagnostics you paste. We use it to answer your request, investigate a bug, or handle a privacy/deletion request. Our support inbox is a Google Gmail account, so Google processes the message as our email provider.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information. If we need to verify a request, we will ask only for what is necessary. We keep support messages until the request is resolved, then delete or anonymize them unless we need them for a legal claim or the law requires retention. You can ask us to delete a support message at any time.

We may disclose a support message if required by law, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a merger or acquisition. If that happens, we will give notice where we can.

9. This website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not set cookies, use analytics, run advertising, embed videos or maps, or load fonts or images from third-party servers. The fonts, icons and screenshots are served from this site.

GitHub may process standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) to serve the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse. We do not receive an analytics dashboard or a list of visitors. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The contact links open your own email app. Nothing is submitted to this website. If we add a form or analytics later, we will update this policy and add consent where required before it goes live.

12. International transfers

Your game save stays on your device. If you email us, your message is stored in Google's Gmail service. Ad data is processed by Google. Website requests are processed by GitHub. Google and GitHub are global companies and may process data in the United States and other countries.

Where required, these providers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions or their own binding corporate rules. You can read the transfer information in the Google Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

13. Retention

  • Game data: until you use Reset progress or uninstall. Reset keeps settings, the player ID and the purchases block; uninstall removes the app's local data. Device backups follow your OS backup settings and retention.
  • Age band: until Reset progress or uninstall. The birth date is never stored.
  • Support messages: until the request is resolved, then deleted or anonymized unless we need them for a legal claim or the law requires retention.
  • Advertising data: determined by Google; see Google's privacy policy.
  • Website logs: determined by GitHub; we do not receive an analytics copy.

14. Security

The game runs in your device's app sandbox. Your save is protected by the device's own access controls and encryption if you have a passcode or biometric lock enabled. Ad requests and this website use HTTPS in transit. Our support inbox is protected by Google account security, including strong authentication.

No method is completely secure. Email is not end-to-end encrypted, so please do not send sensitive information. If you find a security problem, contact us at octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Security”. We will investigate and tell you what we find.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of these rights:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Receive a portable copy of information you provided.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising a privacy right.

Because the game has no account and no server-side profile, most requests are about a support email you sent us. Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Tell us what you want and the email address you used. We may ask for enough information to verify the request, but not more than necessary. We respond within 30 days, or within the period the law requires, and we do not charge a fee unless the law allows it.

For local game data, you can exercise deletion yourself with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app. We cannot do it remotely because we cannot access your device.

16. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: use Settings → Privacy choices where available, or the consent form shown in the game. You can change your mind later.
  • iOS tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Turning it off does not remove ads; it stops cross-app tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device). You can reset or delete the advertising ID.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app. See Delete your data.
  • Delete a support email: email us with the subject “Privacy request”.
  • Website: this site has no cookies or analytics, so there is nothing to opt out of here.

17. California notice

If you are a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA gives you the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell personal information for money.

Categories we or our ad partner may process: identifiers (IP address, device and advertising identifiers), internet or network activity (ad interactions), geolocation (approximate location inferred from IP), and diagnostics/performance data about ads. We do not collect your name, postal address, phone number, precise location, health data, biometric data or financial information. We only receive your email address if you send us a message.

Sharing for personalized advertising. Some personalized advertising may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out by declining personalized ads in Settings → Privacy choices (where available), declining ATT on iOS, or emailing us. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 without affirmative authorization; our child treatment is non-personalized.

Global Privacy Control. This website has no cookies, analytics or sale/sharing, so there is no website data to opt out of. The game does not read a browser GPC signal; use the in-app choices or email us.

Authorized agents. You may use an authorized agent. We may ask for proof of authorization and may verify the request with you. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a right.

18. Other regions

If you are in Brazil, Canada, Australia, India, Japan, South Korea, China or another region with privacy laws, you can contact us at octabytes.studio@gmail.com. We will apply the rights that the law gives you. This policy is a description of our practices, not a claim that we comply with every privacy regime in every country.

19. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, add a short notice at the top of this page. Because there are no accounts, we cannot email you about changes; check this page before relying on a detail. If we add accounts, purchases or a new data practice, we will update this policy before that feature goes live.

20. Contact

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: octabytes.studio@gmail.com (subject “Privacy request”). Support and bug reports: octabytes.studio@gmail.com (subject “Support”). We will reply as soon as we can; privacy requests are handled within 30 days or the period the law requires.

If you are in the EEA or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local supervisory authority. If you are in California, you can contact the California Attorney General. We would rather hear from you first.
