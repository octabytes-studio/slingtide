1. Email us

The fastest way to reach us is email. There is no phone line, no live chat and no contact form that silently drops messages.

Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com

We aim to reply within 5 business days. Privacy and deletion requests are handled within 30 days, or within the period the law requires. Complex bug reports can take longer; we will tell you if that happens.

2. Who to ask

Game support

A level will not load, progress looks wrong, audio or haptics misbehave, or you found a crash. Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Support”.

Privacy and data

Access, deletion, correction, objection or a question about the privacy policy. Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”.

Delete your data

The game has no account; local data is deleted with Reset progress or uninstall. The Delete your data page has the exact steps and a request email for support messages.

Security

Found a vulnerability in the game or this site? Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Security”. Please do not publicly disclose it before we have had a chance to fix it.

Press and partnerships

Review keys, press assets or a collaboration idea: octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Press”.

Accessibility

If a setting, control or screen is hard to use, tell us what happened and which device you use. Email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Accessibility”.

3. Report a bug

A useful bug report includes:

  • your device model and OS version;
  • the game version and build number from the Settings footer;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
  • whether it happens every time or only once;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if it helps;
  • the diagnostics text from Settings → Copy diagnostics, if you are comfortable sharing it.

Diagnostics are copied to your clipboard, not sent automatically. Review the text and remove anything you do not want to share. Please do not send passwords, payment details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information.

4. Response time

We are a small team, so we cannot promise an instant reply. We aim to answer support messages within 5 business days. Privacy and deletion requests are handled within 30 days or the period required by law. If a request needs verification, we will ask only for what is necessary.

7. Accessibility

Slingtide includes Reduce Motion, bigger HP pips, a left-handed HUD and colour-assist palettes for red-green and blue-yellow. The game is portrait and one-handed by design. If a control, colour, text size or animation is hard to use, email us with the subject “Accessibility” and tell us what would help. We cannot promise every request, but we read them and use them to prioritise fixes.

This website aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, alt text, reduced-motion support and readable legal text. If you find a barrier, email us. We will fix what we can and tell you what we changed.
