Delete your Slingtide data

Slingtide has no account and no server-side game profile. Your save lives on your device. This page shows how to remove it, and how to ask us to delete a support email.

The short version

  • Slingtide has no account. There is nothing for us to delete on a server.
  • Reset progress clears levels, stars, shells, upgrades, achievements, stats and the age band.
  • Uninstall removes the rest, including settings and the random player ID.
  • Your device backup may keep a copy until it is overwritten or deleted; that is controlled by iOS or Android.
  • If you emailed us, we can delete that support message on request.

Delete data on this device

Open the game, go to Settings → Reset progress, and type ERASE. This is the fastest path and works without contacting us.

1. There is no account

Slingtide does not create an account, login, guest account or cloud profile. It does not have a leaderboard identity or a server-side save. The random player ID in Settings is a local label for diagnostics; it is not an account and it is not sent to us automatically.

Because there is no server account, there is no “delete my account” button in the usual sense. The data is on your device, so the deletion controls are on your device too. We cannot reach into your phone and delete it for you.

2. Reset progress (keeps settings and player ID)

This is the in-app path. It works offline and takes a few seconds.

  1. Open Slingtide.
  2. Tap the Settings gear on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the About group.
  4. Tap Reset progress.
  5. Type ERASE in the confirmation field.
  6. Tap Reset. The game returns to the home screen and shows “Progress reset”.

The confirmation word is case-sensitive and must be exactly ERASE. This is deliberate: the action cannot be undone.

3. What reset deletes and what it keeps

What “Reset progress” changes
DeletedKept
Levels, clears and stars; shells and wallet balance; upgrade levels; loadout choice; achievements and their progress; lifetime stats; recent-run history; onboarding state; coach marks; the derived age band Sound, music, haptics, Reduce Motion, bigger pips, left-handed HUD and colour assist; the random player ID; the purchases block (empty in this version); the profile creation date

Reset does not remove the app, its settings or the player ID. If you want a completely fresh install with a new player ID, uninstall the app and reinstall it.

4. Remove everything (uninstall)

Uninstalling the app removes its private storage, including the save, settings and player ID. The exact labels vary by OS version.

Android

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Slingtide.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage if you want to wipe the data before removing the app.
  3. Go back and tap Uninstall. (You can also long-press the app icon and choose App info → Uninstall.)

iOS

  1. Touch and hold the Slingtide icon on the Home Screen.
  2. Choose Remove App.
  3. Choose Delete App. Do not choose “Offload App” — offloading keeps the app's data.

If you reinstall later, you start with a new local profile unless your device backup restores the old save.

5. Device backups

If you have Android Auto Backup or iCloud backup enabled, a copy of the save may be kept in your device backup after you reset or uninstall. We cannot delete that copy; it belongs to your Apple or Google account.

  • Android: open Settings → System → Backup (or Google → Backup) and manage the backup. You can turn off backup for the device or delete the backup. The exact path varies by manufacturer.
  • iOS/iCloud: open Settings → your Apple Account name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups, or Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone. You can delete the device backup or turn iCloud Backup off.

If you use a computer backup (iTunes/Finder or a manufacturer tool), delete that backup in the tool as well. Backups are controlled by the platform, not by Slingtide.

6. What we hold

We do not hold a game account, save, player ID or age band. The only data we may hold about you is a support email you chose to send us, plus any attachments or diagnostics you included. Our inbox is a Google Gmail account.

We keep support messages until the request is resolved, then delete or anonymize them unless we need them for a legal claim or the law requires retention. You can ask us to delete a message at any time.

7. Email a deletion request

Use the button below or email octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Slingtide data deletion request”. Please include:

  • the email address you used to contact us;
  • what you want deleted (for example, “all support emails from this address”);
  • your country, so we can apply the right law;
  • any other detail that helps us find the message.

Do not send passwords, payment details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information. If we need to verify the request, we will ask only for what is necessary.

Compose the request

8. Timeline

  • Reset progress: immediate. The game returns to the home screen with a fresh progression state.
  • Uninstall: immediate for the app's local storage; the OS may finish cleanup in the background.
  • Device backup: per your backup provider's schedule. Deleting a backup can take up to 24 hours to propagate.
  • Support email: we aim to action a verified request within 30 days, or within the period the law requires. We will confirm by email when it is done.

9. Purchases and subscriptions

The current version of Slingtide has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel. If we add purchases later, deleting the game or its data will not cancel a subscription. You would need to cancel it in the App Store or Google Play. We will update this page before any purchase feature ships.

10. Consequences

  • Reset and uninstall are irreversible. Progress, stars, shells, upgrades and achievements cannot be recovered by us.
  • Reset keeps your settings and player ID; uninstall removes them.
  • Your device backup may keep a copy until it is overwritten or deleted.
  • Deleting a support email does not affect your ability to play the game.

11. Contact

Data deletion and privacy: octabytes.studio@gmail.com (subject “Slingtide data deletion request” or “Privacy request”). General support: octabytes.studio@gmail.com (subject “Support”). See the Privacy Policy for the full data map.
